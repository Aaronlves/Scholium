# Philosophical-Review Submodes

## `full-panel`

Read the complete available work, apply all five review lenses independently, conduct targeted verification, synthesize the findings, and give a version-bound recommendation.

Use for substantial manuscripts, chapters, proposals, or reports when the researcher wants a comprehensive review.

## `rapid-panel`

Apply all five lenses only to blocking and major issues. Limit verification to claims that could materially change the verdict.

Do not imply exhaustive review. State the omitted depth explicitly.

## `single-lens`

Apply one named lens to a bounded target. Do not infer a panel or whole-work verdict from one lens unless the researcher separately requests and supplies the needed scope.

Available official lenses:

- conceptual and methodological;
- argumentative and inferential;
- interpretive and source-fidelity;
- dialectical and contribution;
- exposition and audience.

## `referee-simulation`

Produce a journal-style report only when the researcher requests it or identifies a venue. State the assumed venue and audience standards. Bind the recommendation to the exact manuscript version.

The simulation does not establish submission readiness, venue compliance, likelihood of acceptance, or authorization to submit.

## `regression-review`

Compare an exact revision against an earlier review and classify each prior finding as:

- `resolved`;
- `partly_resolved`;
- `unresolved`;
- `regressed`;
- `superseded`;
- `newly_introduced`.

Do not count changed wording as resolution when the philosophical defect remains.

## Optional scoring

Add scores only when requested. Use whole-number ordinal judgments and explain each with reasons. Do not use fixed lens weights, arithmetic aggregation, decimal pseudo-precision, or a score to override a decisive objection.
