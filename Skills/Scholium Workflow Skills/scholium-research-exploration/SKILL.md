# Scholium Research Exploration

Apply `scholium-core-protocol`. Use this official method without requiring any personal skill or Practice.

## Establish the exploration packet

Set:

```text
Mode: explore
Task object: question | debate | domain | candidate topic | research direction
Purpose:
Available material:
Read set:
Write set:
Permission:
Output:
Stop condition:
Durability:
```

Default to `candidate-only` and `ephemeral` unless the researcher authorizes an exact durable target.

## Load the method

Read [references/method.md](references/method.md) completely before substantial exploration.

## Apply selected Philosophical Practices

If an explicit Practice is selected, load only that researcher-owned reference plus `COMPOSITION-RULES.md`, and record its stable ID and package revision; otherwise load no Practice. Compatible Practices are `research-explorer`, `historical-interpreter`, `conceptual-analyst`, and `systematizer`.

## Execute

1. Preserve the researcher's initial wording.
2. Bound the domain, tradition, period, source set, and intended use.
3. Reconstruct the background problem and why the issue became philosophically pressing.
4. Distinguish positions by motivations, commitments, gains, and costs.
5. Test whether apparent tensions are conceptual, methodological, historical, verbal, or genuinely incompatible.
6. Explain the stakes of candidate questions without claiming novelty or importance as fact.
7. Generate a small number of source- or reason-grounded directions.
8. End with the smallest useful next research step.

## Return

```text
Initial question and scope:
Background problem:
Why the issue matters:
Positions, motivations, gains, and costs:
Conceptual or argumentative tensions:
Candidate directions and their risks:
Evidence gaps and uncertainties:
Bounded next step:
Researcher decisions needed:
```

Keep proposals explicitly provisional. Do not write to settled notes unless the task packet authorizes the exact target.
