# Structured Critique

Use selectively for a Whole critique.

## Scope

- Work identity and fingerprint
- Whole or Passage scope
- Genre, task, audience, and stage
- Materials and sources inspected
- Limitations

## Charitable map

- Question and controlling claim
- Central concepts and distinctions
- Main route and burden
- Objections, replies, and source roles
- Claimed contribution and verification status

## Assessment

- Strongest contribution
- Version-bound recommendation
- Decisive reasons
- Most serious residual risk

## Prioritized findings

For each finding record severity, locator, Work move, problem, evidence, consequence, strongest repair, cost, residual pressure, and source check.

## Lens differences

Preserve convergent concerns, well-supported minority findings, and repeated findings rejected as shared misreadings. Convergence prioritizes checking; it does not prove a defect.

## Revision burden and decisions

List the smallest ordered burdens, missing verification, and researcher decisions without supplying an unauthorized rewrite.
