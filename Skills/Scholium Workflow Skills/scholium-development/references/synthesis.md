# Conditional Synthesis

Use only across bounded Materials that have already been inspected for the present task.

Fix the research question, corpus boundary, evidential layers, and intended use. Map background, concepts, positions, arguments, objections, replies, cases, source roles, and open questions. Type each important relation narrowly and state its evidence and status.

Do not infer support from co-occurrence, criticism from opposed vocabulary, agreement from similar wording, influence from chronology, or debate participation from a citation. Preserve incompatible but defensible reconstructions and places where compression would create false unity.

The synthesis remains a candidate relation map. It does not authorize changes to Materials or turn a secondary note into primary-source evidence.
