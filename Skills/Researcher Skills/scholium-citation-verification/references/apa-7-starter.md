# APA 7 Starter Rules

This reference supplies a conservative APA 7 starting point. It is not a substitute for the official manual, a venue-specific stylesheet, or source-type-specific verification. When a journal or institution modifies APA, the supplied local rule governs and should be recorded.

## In-text form

- Use author-prominent form when the author is part of the sentence: `Author (Year)`.
- Use parenthetical form otherwise: `(Author, Year)`.
- Give a page or other stable locator for a direct quotation and for a close source-specific paraphrase when checking would materially benefit.
- Distinguish multiple works, same-year works, group authors, and works without an individual author according to the applicable APA rule; do not guess.

## Reference-list form

- Populate only verified bibliographic facts.
- Identify the source type before applying punctuation, capitalization, italics, volume, issue, page-range, editor, edition, or publisher rules.
- Express a verified DOI in current URL form: `https://doi.org/...`.
- Do not invent a DOI, issue number, publisher, location, translator, edition, or page range merely to complete a template.
- Preserve original-language titles and required translations according to the researcher's language and venue policy.

## Completion rule

Before calling an entry APA-compliant, verify:

```text
Source type:
Verified authorship and order:
Verified date:
Verified title and capitalization basis:
Verified container and publication facts:
Verified DOI or URL status:
Applicable venue variation:
Unresolved element:
```

If the exact source-type rule is unavailable, return a clearly labeled provisional entry and identify what must be checked.
