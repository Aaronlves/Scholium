# Academic Prose Control

This editable profile governs meaning-preserving prose realization only. Revision owns substantive architecture and argument repair; selected Practices own optional philosophical lenses. Do not infer a literature judgment, thesis, objection, reply, or conceptual repair from prose preference.

## Paragraph function

Give each paragraph one principal communicative function already supported by the text: context, pressure, claim, distinction, reason, objection, reply, limitation, or transition. Split or reorder only when the semantic and dialectical relations remain unchanged. Do not add a missing reason or consequence merely to make a paragraph look complete.

## Transitions

Make the existing relation between adjacent passages explicit: continuation, contrast, qualification, consequence, objection, reply, concession, or scope shift. A transition must not create an inferential or dialectical relation the original content did not establish.

## Terminology

Preserve source terms, researcher terms, definitions, distinctions, and deliberate variation. Replace vague or inconsistent wording only when the intended referent is determinate. If two readings remain live, expose the ambiguity and withhold a stylistic choice that would settle it.

## Modality and claim strength

Preserve whether a sentence asserts, argues, proposes, suggests, describes, interprets, concedes, or leaves open. Keep necessity, possibility, normativity, generality, uncertainty, and conditions unchanged. Do not use fluency to make a claim stronger, weaker, or more settled than its evidence.

## Source role

Preserve who holds each view and whether the source supplies evidence, an interpretation, a target, a rival, an objection, a case, background, or merely a lead. Do not turn thematic proximity into support or reorganize literature in a way that silently changes those roles.

## Researcher voice

Preserve the researcher's established vocabulary, cadence, first-person commitments, and intended audience unless the current instruction explicitly changes them. Avoid generic agent polish that makes distinct positions sound alike or erases unresolved pressure.

## Semantic-preservation check

Compare original and revision for:

- thesis and local claim;
- concepts, definitions, and distinctions;
- inferential, explanatory, interpretive, and dialectical relations;
- objections, replies, concessions, and residual pressure;
- source attribution, quotation, locator, and evidential role;
- scope, modality, normative force, qualification, and epistemic status;
- paragraph function, transition relation, terminology, and researcher voice.

If any substantive item changed, restore it. Keep a genuinely needed philosophical repair outside the prose revision as a labeled candidate for the researcher.
