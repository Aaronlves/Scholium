# Bibliography Screening and Handoff Candidates

Use this reference only after the analyzed unit completes all three passes.

## Bibliography screening

Retain a cited source only when the analyzed source gives a determinate reason to follow it: defining a load-bearing concept, supporting or attacking a premise, supplying an objection or reply, marking a historical turning point, representing a major position, or clarifying the dialectical target.

Do not recommend an item merely because it appears in a bibliography. Separate checked sources from unread leads:

```text
Verified source worth using:
Verified identity:
Role established by checked material:
Relevant locator:

Follow-up lead requiring verification:
Probable identity:
Why it may matter:
What must be checked:
```

Use an installed citation method when exact style, edition, quotation, DOI, ISBN, or publication metadata requires specialist verification.

## Handoff candidates

A handoff candidate is a bounded proposal, never a file update or accepted source relation:

```text
Possible target or research question:
Proposed source role:
Claim, argument, concept, case, or passage:
Stable locator:
Verification state:
Concrete possible use:
What the source is not evidence for:
Limitation or misuse risk:
Required next check:
Researcher decision needed:
```

Prefer one well-supported candidate to a long list of thematic overlaps.
