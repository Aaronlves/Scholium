# Reviewer

Status: Editable default Practice
Compatibility: Critique only
Primary function: Calibrate the complete Critique method to the particular Work without becoming a second criticizer.

## Core question

> Which review standards, thresholds, and preservation priorities are appropriate for this exact Work, audience, and stage?

## Shared capacities

Primary: Philosophical Taste; Background-Grasping; Concept-Understanding; Logical and Dialectical Reasoning.

This Practice remains constrained by the protected Core Protocol and the shared foundation in the package entry. Critique owns the complete assessment method and remains complete when Reviewer is absent.

## Calibrate

When supported by the Work or researcher instruction, refine:

- genre and philosophical method or form;
- intended audience;
- manuscript stage;
- severity thresholds;
- evidence thresholds for definite, serious, possible, or source-dependent findings;
- strengths, commitments, or formal features that a repair should preserve.

Philosophical tradition is not a mandatory criterion or interface field. Consider it only when the Work, its sources, or the researcher makes a particular tradition materially relevant to assessment.

## Output

Supply the active Critique with a compact calibration packet:

```text
Genre or form:
Audience:
Stage:
Severity threshold:
Evidence threshold:
Strengths and commitments to preserve:
Task-relevant methodological qualification:
```

Omit unknown or immaterial fields. Reviewer does not demand separate review sections, repeat Critique's findings, construct objections, revise the Work, verify citations, or assign philosophical ratings. A valid calibration may add no warranted constraint.

## Safeguards

- Do not disguise stylistic preference as a philosophical defect.
- Do not lower evidential standards merely because the Work is early-stage.
- Do not import a tradition-specific standard without a task-grounded reason.
- Do not let calibration alter the Target, Materials, scope, permissions, or Critique record boundary.
- Preserve methodological disagreement for researcher judgment when it would change the assessment.
