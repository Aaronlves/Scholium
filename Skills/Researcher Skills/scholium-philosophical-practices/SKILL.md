# Scholium Philosophical Practices

Treat this package as researcher-owned methodology. The researcher may edit, combine, add, or retire named Practices. Official Workflow Skills remain complete without this package.

## Shared foundation

The protected Core Protocol remains the sole authority for source fidelity, non-fabrication, researcher authority, scope, and permission. The four shared philosophical capacities below help characterize what a Practice examines; they are not modes, ratings, or guarantees of correctness.

- **Background-Grasping:** understand relevant positions, interlocutors, motivations, commitments, costs, historical pressures, and the difference between genuine disagreement and different questions. Do not reduce background to an author inventory.
- **Concept-Understanding:** preserve and compare meanings, uses, distinctions, ambiguities, and theoretical roles without assuming equivalence or demanding necessary-and-sufficient conditions when another account is more appropriate.
- **Philosophical Taste:** identify and explain potentially significant questions, tensions, consequences, and accounts without treating interest, novelty, or importance as an objective fact.
- **Logical and Dialectical Reasoning:** reconstruct and assess premises, conclusions, assumptions, inferences, objections, replies, burdens, and costs while distinguishing validity, soundness, plausibility, explanatory adequacy, and evidential support.

Every named Practice declares its primary and supporting capacities. Those declarations describe emphasis only.

Do not load the whole library by default. Record the selected stable IDs and exact package and resource revisions. A later Practice edit does not alter an earlier result.

## Compose as supplements

A selected Practice supplements one complete primary method. It may sharpen questions, add criteria, reorder examination, or request a bounded additional output. It cannot replace the primary method, even at one editable point. Legacy `replace` bindings require explicit repair: convert them to supplemental application or select a complete Researcher Skill as the primary method.

Before composing multiple Practices, give each a distinct job. Consider every selected Practice genuinely and allocate methodological effort flexibly according to the task and evidence. Central lenses may require extensive examination; secondary lenses may supply a safeguard check. A valid Practice can yield no warranted finding.

Do not:

- assign numerical weights, coverage ratios, or priority labels;
- expose an allocation ledger;
- silently skip a selected Practice;
- manufacture prose, criticism, distinctions, or arguments to make coverage look balanced;
- let the last-loaded Practice win merely because of load order.

When two Practices differ materially, preserve the disagreement, identify its methodological source, explain how the choice would affect the result, and leave the substantive choice to the researcher. Do not average incompatible standards into false consensus.

## Preserve the boundary

Practice selection cannot change the Target, Materials, task scope, permissions, checkpoint requirements, write set, or durability. A Practice is not evidence and cannot weaken source-fidelity or non-fabrication requirements. Report a Practice only when it materially influenced the procedure or result; otherwise a concise `no warranted finding` is sufficient.
