# Source-Analysis Report Templates

Use a structure proportionate to the request, source, and interpretive risk. The template controls organization, not evidential status.

## Thorough report

```markdown
# Author (Year): Short Title

## Metadata and Access
- Verified reference and version:
- Source type and function:
- Language or translation:
- Materials checked and unavailable:
- Selectable-text and pagination reliability:

## Analytical Scope
- Session analysis unit:
- Orientation pass:
- Analytical pass:
- Review pass:
- Material limitations:

## Source Project
- Question or pressure:
- Thesis, purpose, or contribution:
- Method, structure, and scope:
- Authorial debate framing:

## Concepts and Distinctions

## Arguments or Interpretive Moves
For each central unit, label source-explicit material, reconstruction, repair, and evaluation separately and provide stable locators.

## Objections, Replies, and Residual Pressure

## Evaluation and Unresolved Alternatives

## Open Questions and Next Checks
```

When the researcher requests later-use handoffs or bibliography screening and the analysis warrants them, append only the relevant structured output defined in `later-use-handoffs.md` or `bibliography-recommendations.md`. Omit either section when it was not requested or when there are zero warranted entries. Recommended Bibliography belongs in Scholium's structured Research record, not in the analysis note, unless the researcher explicitly asks for a prose rendering.

## Concise report

```markdown
# Author (Year): Short Title

## Access and Scope

## Gist and Source Function

## Central Concepts and Structure

## Strongest Warranted Unresolved Problem, If Any

## Possible Use or Next Check
```

Concise output still requires all three source passes unless the researcher explicitly requests a bounded preliminary scan.

## Provisional report

Use for an abstract, excerpt, unreliable OCR, or partial source:

```markdown
# Provisional Analysis: Author (Year): Short Title

## Available and Missing Material

## Exact Unit Reviewed

## What Can Responsibly Be Said

## What Cannot Yet Be Claimed

## Provisional Reconstruction

## Verification Needed
```

Do not claim a full argument, exact quotation, complete source role, or whole-source conclusion from partial material.
