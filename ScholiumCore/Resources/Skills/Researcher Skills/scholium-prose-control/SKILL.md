# Scholium Prose Control

Apply `scholium-core-protocol` and the `scholium-write` permission boundary. Treat this package as an editable, researcher-owned specialist method. It controls how existing philosophical content is expressed; it does not decide what the researcher should believe or silently repair the philosophy.

An adopted copy belongs to the researcher. The researcher may replace its style models, priorities, examples, and output preferences, and Scholium releases must not overwrite that copy.

## Establish the prose-control packet

```text

```

Selection of Prose Control does not grant file-edit permission. Follow the current Write packet, checkpoint, exact Target, and write range.

## Record the preservation ledger

Before revising, identify the original passage's commitments:

```text
Thesis, conclusions, and local claim:
Claims and claim strength:
Concepts, definitions, and distinctions:
Inferential, explanatory, interpretive, and dialectical relations:
Objections, replies, concessions, and residual pressure:
Source attributions, quotations, and evidential roles:
Scope, modality, normativity, qualification, and status:
Researcher voice and terminology that must remain stable:
Material ambiguity or defect that must remain visible:
```

Treat every populated item as a semantic invariant. If the passage supports two plausible ledgers, do not choose one through stylistic revision; state the ambiguity and request or propose the smallest clarification.

## Load the researcher-owned method

Read [references/academic-prose-style.md](references/academic-prose-style.md) completely before a substantial revision. Treat it as an editable starting profile rather than a universal Scholium standard. Use a named exemplar only when it belongs to the authorized read set and its relevant prose feature has been checked directly.

## Execute

1. Read the exact target in enough surrounding context to identify its local philosophical function.
2. Build the preservation ledger from the text and explicit researcher instruction; do not infer a stronger intended thesis because it would be easier to express.
3. Diagnose the communicative problem: question visibility, paragraph function, order, contrast, burden mapping, transition, redundancy, ambiguity, rhythm, terminology, claim modesty, or audience calibration.
4. Revise only the communicative realization, using the selected style profile where applicable.
5. Compare the revision against the original line by line and against every ledger item.
6. Restore any accidental substantive change. Keep a genuinely needed philosophical repair outside the controlled revision as a labeled candidate.
7. Reread the revised passage in context and verify that its philosophical movement is clearer without becoming stronger, weaker, more certain, more complete, or differently attributed.
8. For an authorized file edit, preserve unrelated Markdown, YAML, links, citations, formatting, comments, and custom fields exactly; use the current fingerprint and supported Scholium mutation path.

## Intervention boundary

Prose Control may:

- remove redundancy and repair ambiguous syntax when the intended meaning is determinate;
- improve sentence and paragraph unity;
- reorder material within the exact target when logical or dialectical relations remain unchanged;
- make existing contrasts, burdens, qualifications, and transitions more visible;
- improve signposting, rhythm, plainness, and audience calibration;
- replace vague wording with more precise wording only when the established content is preserved;

Prose Control may not silently:

- add, remove, strengthen, weaken, complete, or reconcile a claim;
- invent or revise a concept, definition, distinction, premise, example, objection, reply, or concession;
- repair an invalid inference or unsupported interpretation;
- alter a source attribution, quotation, locator, evidential role, or literature claim;
- resolve a live ambiguity or disagreement;
- change scope, modality, normative force, qualification, confidence, or research status;
- turn an agent proposal into researcher-authored or settled content.

Clarity may expose a substantive defect. It must not conceal the defect behind fluent prose.

## Return

For a substantial revision, return:

```text
Revised prose or exact change:
What the preservation ledger protected:
Communicative improvements:
Substantive problems deliberately left unchanged:
Candidate philosophical repairs kept separate:
Source or wording checks still needed:
Durability and fingerprint status:
```

Lead with the revised prose or academic change, not an operation log. Do not describe the result as philosophically improved merely because it is clearer. If the revision may have altered a substantive or source-dependent commitment, treat that as a failed preservation check, restore the earlier meaning, and report the unresolved choice.
