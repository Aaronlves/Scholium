# Revision Method

## Fix function and preservation

State whether the authorized change must orient, motivate, define, distinguish, reconstruct, interpret, describe, claim, argue, object, reply, concede, limit, compare, synthesize, or transition. Identify the researcher's intended thesis, the Work's wider burden, source roles, terminology, uncertainty, and intervention boundary.

Flag any proposed change to thesis, conclusion, definition, scope, modality, burden, concession, objection-reply structure, source attribution, source role, or research status. Do not hide substantive change inside smoother prose.

## Repair substance before prose

For a substantial revision:

1. identify what the reader must understand;
2. identify what the passage must establish, interpret, describe, or explain;
3. identify premises, passages, cases, values, or explanatory considerations;
4. make the inferential, interpretive, explanatory, formal, or normative link visible;
5. stabilize scope, modality, contrast class, and definitions;
6. test the strongest relevant pressure or rival reading;
7. reply, concede, distinguish, narrow, or leave the pressure explicitly unresolved;
8. explain how the revision advances the Work.

Calibrate to genre. Historical work must distinguish text, interpretation, rival reading, context, and current significance. Phenomenological work must separate description from later explanation or normativity. Formal work must connect definitions and derivation back to the philosophical claim. Normative work must expose force, bridges, defeat, feasibility, and residual demand. Empirically informed work must separate result, warranted scope, bridge premise, and philosophical consequence.

## Draft and revise

Verify load-bearing source support early. Repair logical or interpretive structure, then section and paragraph order, then scope and terminology, then citation form and surface prose. Preserve the researcher's voice without imitating or replacing it. Do not invent a quotation, locator, bibliographic fact, premise, or transition.

If two plausible revisions express different commitments, keep both as candidates and request the researcher's decision. If an unavailable source is load-bearing, narrow or stop.

## Write boundary

Edit only the exact current Work and authorized range. Preserve unrelated Markdown, YAML, links, citations, comments, ordering, line endings, and final newline. Materials are never writable. A later dependent Work change requires a new function run.

The completion handoff must identify the final fingerprint and whether source-dependent or substantive changes require Content Fidelity, Citations, or both. A successful write is not an audit result.
