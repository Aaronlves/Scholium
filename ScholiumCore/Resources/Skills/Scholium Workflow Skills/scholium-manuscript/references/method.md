# Manuscript Coordination Method

## Diagnose the stage

Identify the immediate burden without imposing one linear path: question and contribution; source basis; conceptual or argumentative development; architecture and drafting; local revision; exact-version Fidelity; independent Critique; received-feedback revision; or researcher decision.

Use the smallest ordinary function that advances the real burden. A later phase may expose a reason to revisit an earlier one.

## Isolate phases

Every phase must declare purpose, function, Target fingerprint, Materials and fingerprints, read set, write set, permission, package and resources, output, stop condition, durability, and handoff status. The write set is always a subset of the current Work authorization and is empty outside Revise.

Reset assumptions and method between phases. Revision performs any conceptual, argumentative, feedback-responsive, or expository development that belongs to the Work within its own permission. Fidelity tests the final output rather than validating the Revision agent's intention. Critique reconstructs independently and writes only its separate record.

When the same final fingerprint and audit key already have completed Fidelity, reuse that outcome. A later Target or evidence change makes the earlier result stale. Never recursively schedule Fidelity from inside Fidelity.

## Preserve authorship and evidence

Keep source claims, researcher claims, agent proposals, Comments, Dialogue, Critique findings, and received feedback distinct. Preserve the researcher's intended thesis during authorized revision. Mark novelty, significance, completeness, and literature-placement claims unverified unless checked.

A structurally complete manuscript may still have missing evidence. A Fidelity pass does not entail a favorable Critique. A favorable Critique does not constitute Human Review or researcher acceptance.

## Stop conditions

Stop when the Work or Material fingerprint changes, required source evidence is unavailable, a phase would write outside the current Work, a style-specific citation check lacks a compatible binding, a substantive handoff needs researcher adoption, or an external action would exceed the request.
