# Manuscript Gates

Assess each gate with separate evidence state and judgment, bound to the exact Work fingerprint.

## Question and contribution

The question, stakes, thesis, scope, and contribution are explicit. Novelty and literature-placement claims identify their verification basis.

## Source basis

Load-bearing attributions, quotations, interpretations, locators, and source roles are checked or visibly unresolved. Citation style is checked only by a compatible bound Researcher Skill.

## Concept and argument

Central concepts and distinctions are stable. Premises, bridges, conclusions, objections, replies, concessions, scope, and residual burdens are inspectable.

## Architecture and exposition

Each section performs a necessary philosophical function. The reader can distinguish exposition, interpretation, reconstruction, criticism, and the researcher's position.

## Exact-version integrity

The final substantive fingerprint has completed the selected Fidelity checks. Blockers and major findings are resolved, accepted as explicit limitations, or waiting for researcher decision.

## Independent criticism and feedback

An independent Critique has been considered when appropriate. Actual received feedback has explicit dispositions; accepted items have bounded revisions and regression checks.

## Researcher and venue decision

Current venue instructions, anonymization, length, reference style, permissions, disclosures, and artifact format are checked when relevant without exposing private prose in public queries. Only the researcher decides whether to circulate or submit.

The strongest agent statement is ready for researcher submission decision. It is never approved for submission or publication ready.
