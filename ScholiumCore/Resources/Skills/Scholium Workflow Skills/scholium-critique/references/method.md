# Critique Method

## Calibrate

Identify whether the Work defends, interprets, distinguishes, explains, diagnoses, recovers, dissolves, synthesizes, applies, or reframes. Calibrate to argument-driven, historical, phenomenological, genealogical, formal, normative, conceptual-engineering, case-based, empirically informed, or hybrid work. State any standard not supplied by the Work or researcher.

Whole scope may assess the complete stable Work. Passage scope assesses only the selected passage in enough surrounding context to judge its local function. Do not extrapolate a whole-Work verdict from a passage.

## Build the charitable map

Reconstruct the question, aim, thesis or controlling interpretation, concepts, scope, method, grounds, inferential or interpretive route, objections, replies, source roles, and claimed contribution. Label any premise or argument supplied as a charitable repair rather than attributing it to the Work.

## Apply independent lenses

Record each lens before synthesis so one diagnosis does not seed every other lens:

- conceptual and methodological: thesis, definitions, distinctions, scope, modality, ambiguity, method fit, overbreadth, and underbreadth;
- argumentative and inferential: premises, bridges, inference, force, burden, hidden assumptions, objection, reply, and residual pressure;
- interpretive and source-fidelity: passages, context, edition or translation, chronology, rival readings, source terminology, and evidential roles;
- dialectical and contribution: organizing pressure, relevant alternatives, burden discharge, contribution type, significance, limits, and material omissions;
- exposition and audience: whether the reader can identify the problem, route, claims, burdens, section functions, terminology, and transitions after substance is assessed.

Do not force every philosophical method into deduction or treat novelty as the only contribution.

## Verify and adjudicate

Verify a finding when it materially depends on attribution, interpretation, literature placement, quotation, historical context, or an empirical premise. Prefer inspected primary texts and stable passages. Notes and earlier agent responses establish researcher context, not published-source claims.

For each finding state severity, exact target, the Work's move, pressure, internal or external status, evidence, consequence, strongest reply or repair, cost, residual pressure, and missing source check.

Use BLOCKER only when the central aim fails with no plausible local repair; MAJOR for a central burden; MODERATE for a significant local burden; MINOR for a localized correction; SUGGESTION for an optional improvement. Do not invent findings to fill a quota.

## Boundaries

The Critique may recommend no major revision, limited revision, major revision, fundamental reconsideration, or unverified for the exact scope and version. It must not revise the Work, record Human Review, assert Fidelity, accept a Comment, or change readiness state.
