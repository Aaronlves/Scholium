# Citation Fidelity Coordination

Citation work has three separable questions:

1. bibliographic identity: whether the author, title, edition, publication facts, DOI or ISBN, and cited object are correct;
2. evidential support: whether the exact source passage supports the claim, quotation, paraphrase, or locator assigned to it;
3. formatting: whether the citation and reference conform to the researcher-selected style and source type.

The official Fidelity package may coordinate these checks but does not supply a universal style. Application must provide one explicitly bound Triptych-local Researcher Skill with declared capabilities. Citation verification requires citation-verification. Style conversion additionally requires citation-formatting and an exact citation style compatible with the request.

Load only the bound package and resources selected for this run. Record its whole-package revision, loaded resource revisions, declared capabilities, and style. Do not substitute APA 7 for Chicago, MLA, Oxford, MHRA, a journal house style, legal citation, ancient-text conventions, or any unlisted style.

If the source, edition, locator, or bibliographic facts cannot be inspected, mark the relevant item unverified. Formatting cannot cure uncertain metadata or unsupported claims. Bibliographic metadata alone cannot establish philosophical support.

Return atomic findings that identify the citation, claim or quotation, source and edition, locator, verification state, formatting state, and smallest correction. Do not modify the Target during Fidelity.
