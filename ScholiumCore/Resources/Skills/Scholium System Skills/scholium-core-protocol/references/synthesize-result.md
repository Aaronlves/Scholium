# Synthesize Result composition

Use the frozen Synthesize Result Contract. For the current default fields:

- **Synthesis Outcome** receives the Method's controlling synthesis judgment,
  its necessary reason, and any qualification needed to understand it. Do not
  duplicate the saved Topic or divide this field into one report per Material.
  Compression must preserve distinctions among independent explanatory axes,
  levels, or burdens; do not turn a cross-cutting model into a rival at the
  wrong level merely to obtain a short taxonomy. When one top-level question
  divides into independent standards or subdimensions that control the
  synthesis, preserve that hierarchy in the Outcome rather than treating an
  umbrella term as sufficient.
- **Contribution** declares whether the result adds, corrects, qualifies,
  connects, reopens, or warrants no change. No-warranted-change is exclusive.
  Use `reopens` only when the update makes a previously closed, excluded, or
  settled issue live again, not merely because it develops an already open
  question.
- **Unresolved Tension**, when present, contains only a tension not already
  expressed in the outcome.
- **Next Step**, when present, contains only a bounded next research decision
  or investigation warranted by the synthesis.

Compose every field from the final validated Topic hierarchy, not from a
source-by-source recap. Before submission, compare each Result sentence with
the saved Topic. The Outcome cannot omit a controlling subdimension merely for
brevity. An Unresolved Tension cannot introduce an equivalence, opposition, or
binary between source-relative terms that the Topic has not established, and
cannot narrow a proposal's scope. Keep separate tensions separate when no
bridge warrants combining them. Do not call an item an architecture or peer
configuration when the Topic classifies it as a cross-cutting overlay,
constraint, analogy, or process model. Do not join source-relative terms with
a slash, “or,” or synonym-like pairing unless the Topic establishes their
equivalence; name their relation or keep them separate.

Explicit frozen Material identities and exact changed documents travel through
Application provenance, not as repeated academic prose. Reading history and
source-use testimony are not submitted. Do not narrate searches, reads,
comparison stages, independent reverification, or file changes. State a
scholarly limitation as the bounded epistemic scope of the result, not as what
the Agent did or did not read, compare, or verify.
