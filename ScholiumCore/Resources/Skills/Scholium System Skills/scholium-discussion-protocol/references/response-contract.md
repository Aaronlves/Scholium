# Discussion response contract

Use only the immutable response contract in the exact Discussion record. A
Triptych default may seed a future Discussion but never replaces an existing
snapshot. A missing or unsupported snapshot fails closed; do not infer modules
from the question, research evidence, the Discuss Method, or a newer default.

## Required outcome

Begin with one concise Academic Outcome stating the bounded scholarly result,
partial result, or research consequence of a blocker. Routine commands and
file operations are not an academic outcome.

Then consider every selected module in its stored order:

- Critical Reflection: the strongest warranted assumption, weakness, tension,
  counterexample, interpretive risk, or cost.
- Remaining Questions: only directly relevant questions still open after this
  exchange.
- Philosophical Significance: the conditional conceptual, argumentative,
  methodological, or practical stake.
- Debate Context: the supported relation to relevant positions, motivations,
  commitments, or costs.
- Research Directions: bounded next investigation tied to a specific gap or
  pressure.

Compose the selected modules as one coherent philosophical response. They are
presentation concerns, not mandatory headings or separate mini-reports. Give
only warranted, nonduplicative content; if a module yields no distinct point,
omit it rather than inventing filler or announcing an empty category. Preserve
source limits, uncertainty, conflicts, failed persistence, and researcher
decisions regardless of selected modules.

## Presentation boundary

Honor the snapshot's concision and Comment-preservation choice. Condensation
changes presentation only: preserve the intellectual request, objection,
qualification, or decision required by that choice, and never present a
synthesis as a verbatim researcher turn. The response contract changes
presentation, not evidence, retrieval, philosophical method, or write
authority.
