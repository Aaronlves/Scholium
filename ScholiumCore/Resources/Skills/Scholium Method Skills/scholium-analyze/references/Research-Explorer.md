# Research Explorer

Status: Bundled philosophical lens reference
Primary function: Discover and sharpen philosophical questions, stakes, candidate gaps, and bounded research directions without inventing a researcher position or treating incomplete coverage as field knowledge.

## Core question

> What is philosophically at stake, which questions are worth investigating, and what evidence would distinguish a genuine gap from absence in the current materials?

## Shared capacities

Primary: Philosophical Taste; Background-Grasping.
Supporting: Concept-Understanding; Logical and Dialectical Reasoning.

This lens remains constrained by the current Skill and the protected Research Action protocol.

## Entry conditions

- A starting interest, domain, question, tension, or bounded source set is identifiable.
- The intended research use and practical limits are known or can be stated provisionally.
- Claims about field state can be verified or explicitly withheld.

An absent researcher position requests neutral orientation. Map plausible questions, positions, and costs without manufacturing a thesis, preferred camp, or project commitment.

## Procedure

1. Clarify the starting interest without prematurely fixing a final question.
2. Separate authorial self-positioning, the researcher's existing map, the agent's reconstruction, and independently verified field context.
3. Map the available positions by motivations, methods, commitments, gains,
   costs, and genuine points of disagreement.
4. Formulate a small set of candidate questions or problem statements and
   explain their philosophical pressure.
5. For a question the researcher wants to pursue, propose a bounded research
   design using the operating form below. Method choice is question-relative;
   do not make one philosophical method the universal default.
6. Test scope, tractability, available evidence, risks of triviality, selection
   bias, and alternative explanations.
7. For any proposed research gap, complete the gap record below.
8. When judging whether to continue searching or constructing, complete a
   provisional stopping record for each material activity rather than making
   one global completeness claim.
9. Suggest one bounded next investigation for each live pressure; leave topic,
   method, thesis, and stopping decisions to the researcher.

## Research design operating form

```text
Governing question and bounded subquestions:
Why the question matters, provisionally:
Candidate method or method combination:
Why those inputs and procedures fit the question:
Success conditions and what they cannot establish:
Material and search basis:
Inclusion, exclusion, language, tradition, and cutoff limits:
Relevant cases, interpretations, disciplines, or perspectives:
Selection-bias and positionality risks:
Counter-search or discriminating test:
What would revise or defeat the design:
Next bounded investigation:
```

Separate method from methodology: a method is a procedure used in the inquiry;
methodology explains which inputs, procedures, and success conditions are
appropriate and why. Treat both as revisable. Philosophical work may require
tacit judgment and creativity, but those do not excuse leaving load-bearing
choices unexplained.

## Gap Detection operating form

Every proposed gap records:

```text
Object and scope:
Gap kind:
Status: author-declared | analyst-inferred | project-coverage | field-unverified | field-supported
Evidence and search basis actually checked:
Why it matters:
Counterevidence or alternative explanation:
What would falsify the claim:
Remaining verification:
One bounded next investigation:
```

Use `author-declared` only for the author's explicit self-positioning, not as proof that the field has the gap. Use `analyst-inferred` for a pressure reconstructed from checked material. Use `project-coverage` for absence from the researcher's bounded materials. Use `field-unverified` for a candidate field claim lacking a sufficient search basis.

Use `field-supported` only when the record documents the repositories or
corpora checked, exact queries, languages, cutoff dates, inclusion basis, and a
reasonable counter-search for terminology or perspective bias. The Agent must
also inspect the relevant results and enough representative field material to
construct a bounded account of the live positions, terminology, and known
exceptions against which the apparent absence is judged. An empty result set,
search count, or repeated query alone never supports a field gap. The status
remains a bounded, defeasible field claim, never proof of exhaustive absence.
Otherwise say `candidate gap` or `absent from the current materials`. A partial
background map is not a field map.

## Provisional stopping operating form

Assess each applicable activity separately: literature search; cases;
interpretations or perspectives; reflective pressure; and theory construction.

```text
Activity and bounded question:
Recent additions that materially changed the map:
What is now repeating rather than refining it:
Counter-search or bias check attempted:
Known exclusions and access limits:
State: not-assessed | insufficient-basis | advancing | practical-cutoff | provisionally-saturated | blocked-or-stuck
Reason for that state:
Defeating material that would reopen the activity:
One bounded next probe, if any:
```

Use `not-assessed` when stopping adequacy was not examined. Use
`insufficient-basis` when the Agent lacks an actual sequence of additions to
compare, enough inspected material, or a substantive bias-oriented
counter-search. `Provisionally-saturated` is available only when an inspected
sequence of recent additions has stopped changing the relevant map and the
counter-search has been attempted. It is gradual, local, and defeasible.
`Practical-cutoff` names time, access, language, or scope limits.
`Blocked-or-stuck` means the inquiry is not progressing without evidence that
it has been adequately tested. None of these states establishes complete
literature coverage, field consensus, or a finished research project.

## Output contract

Return only sections useful to the task:

- neutral orientation or the researcher's stated position;
- candidate philosophical questions;
- positions, motivations, commitments, and costs;
- stakes, tensions, and assumptions;
- structured candidate-gap records;
- bounded research-design and provisional-stopping records when requested;
- risks, missing evidence, and bounded next investigations.

No proposed gap, novelty claim, or research direction becomes a researcher decision merely because the agent recommends it.

## Safeguards

- Do not treat interest, difficulty, or a paper's self-description as proof of importance or novelty.
- Do not convert missing citations or absence from selected Materials into field absence.
- Do not invent a mature researcher position when none exists.
- Do not select the researcher's topic or thesis on the researcher's behalf.
- Do not expand a bounded inquiry merely to make the result look comprehensive.
- Do not confuse repetition in one search vocabulary, corpus, language, or
  tradition with adequate coverage.
- Do not confuse being stuck or reaching a deadline with provisional
  saturation.
- A valid run may find no warranted gap.

## Editable points

The researcher may specify fields, source limits, languages, traditions,
cutoff dates, feasibility constraints, significance standards, admissible
methods, success criteria, bias checks, stopping conditions, or whether
exploration should remain broad or tightly bounded.
