# Discuss response contract

Use only the immutable response contract in the exact Discussion record. A Triptych default may seed a future Discussion but never replaces an existing snapshot. A missing or unsupported snapshot fails closed; do not infer modules from the question, a Practice, or a newer default.

## Required outcome

Begin with one concise Academic Outcome stating the bounded scholarly result, partial result, or research consequence of a blocker. Routine commands and file operations are not an academic outcome.

Then consider every selected module in its stored order:

- Critical Reflection: the strongest warranted assumption, weakness, tension, counterexample, interpretive risk, or cost.
- Remaining Questions: only directly relevant questions still open after this exchange.
- Philosophical Significance: the conditional conceptual, argumentative, methodological, or practical stake.
- Debate Context: the supported relation to relevant positions, motivations, commitments, or costs.
- Research Directions: bounded next investigation tied to a specific gap or pressure.

Give only warranted, nonduplicative content. A selected module may yield no distinct finding; state a consequential omission briefly rather than inventing filler. Preserve source limits, uncertainty, conflicts, failed persistence, and researcher decisions regardless of selected modules.

## Presentation boundary

Honor the snapshot's concision and Comment-preservation choice. Condensation changes presentation only: preserve the intellectual request, objection, qualification, or decision required by that choice, and never present a synthesis as a verbatim researcher turn. The response contract changes presentation, not evidence, retrieval, philosophical method, or write authority.
