# Content Fidelity

Apply `scholium-core-protocol`. If no authenticated Run exists, enter through
its project-discovery route before applying this Skill.

Audit the exact Target revision read-only. This method checks whether content remains faithful to its claimed basis; it does not supply a peer Critique, improve prose, or certify truth.

## Checks

When Content is selected, read `references/content.md`. Separate source-text fidelity, conceptual accuracy, argument reconstruction, evidential role, and unsupported extrapolation. A missing or inaccessible source yields unavailable or unverified, never a guessed pass.

When Citations is selected, read `references/citations.md` and the exact explicitly bound citation package. Check claims atomically: metadata, quotation or paraphrase, locator, source support, and requested style. Do not infer a citation method from filenames or global tools.

## Philosophical lenses

Philosophical lenses are methodologically substantive references within this
Skill, not separate authority objects. Read the smallest applicable set:

- `references/Argument-Reconstructionist.md` when fidelity turns on whether a
  premise, inference, objection, or argumentative role was represented
  accurately;
- `references/Conceptual-Analyst.md` when fidelity turns on a concept,
  distinction, definition, use, or conceptual dependency;
- `references/Historical-Interpreter.md` when a historical or textual claim
  must remain distinct from later reconstruction or evaluation.

Once selected, a lens's evidential distinctions and safeguards are part of
the check; it cannot widen the selected Content or Citations scope.

## Outcome

Report findings against the checked revision and evidence set. State access limits and distinguish verified, provisional, disputed, and unavailable claims. Do not edit the Target, mutate citations, or convert the result into researcher acceptance.
