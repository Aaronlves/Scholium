# Citation-fidelity method

Use only the explicitly bound citation package and style resource recorded for this run. Never infer a style or verification authority from a filename, global configuration, or familiar-looking citation.

Check each citation as separate questions:

1. Is the cited object identified correctly, including authorship, title, edition or version, publication facts, and stable identifiers when available?
2. Is quoted or paraphrased wording accurate and clearly distinguished?
3. Does the locator identify the inspected passage in the cited edition or pagination scheme?
4. Does that passage support the exact claim and strength assigned to it?
5. Does the citation and reference express the verified facts in the selected style?

Formatting cannot repair uncertain identity, a missing source, an inaccurate quotation, or unsupported philosophical use. Return one attributed finding per defective or unavailable item and propose the smallest correction. Do not mutate the Target during Check Fidelity.
