# Critique method

## Establish the object

Bind the assessment to the supplied Work revision and whole-Work or passage scope. Reconstruct the relevant claim and its role before evaluating it.

## Independent lenses

Use only lenses that bear on the actual object:

- conceptual precision and stable definitions;
- argument validity, premise support, and inferential gaps;
- strongest objection, strongest available reply, residual pressure, and limits;
- source fidelity, source coverage, and missing evidence;
- alternative positions, cases, or framings;
- architecture, priority, and likely downstream consequence.

Do not force a criticism under every lens. Preserve genuine strengths and live alternatives.

## Research burdens for a whole Work

Use these as conditional burdens, not a score or universal template:

- **Question and contribution:** Is the governing question precise, and is the
  proposed contribution distinguishable from exposition or recombination?
  Does the Work avoid claiming novelty without an adequate field basis?
- **Method fit and transparency:** Are the inputs, procedure, bridge premises,
  and success conditions appropriate to the philosophical undertaking and
  visible where a reader needs them? Do not prefer one method merely because it
  resembles another field's validation practice.
- **Literature and perspective:** Is the source basis proportionate to the
  Work's actual coverage claim? Are material exclusions, selection bias,
  competing interpretations, and relevant traditions or disciplines addressed
  or bounded explicitly?
- **Conceptual and argumentative command:** Are definitions, distinctions,
  premises, cases, objections, replies, and implications connected across the
  whole Work rather than locally plausible but globally inconsistent?
- **Sustained contribution:** Does each major part discharge a necessary burden
  of the controlling claim, and can the Work explain its significance and
  limits without oversimplification?
- **Defensibility:** Are the strongest live objection or rival interpretation,
  the best available reply, residual pressure, and consequences for revision
  explicit?

Judge each burden only against inspected evidence and the Work's stated genre,
scope, and audience. A coherent or sophisticated Work can still lack the
external evidence needed for a novelty or coverage claim. A weak or missing
burden is a finding for the checked revision, not a level judgment about the
researcher. Never certify a Work as original, publishable, doctoral-level, or
complete.

## Finding form

For each material finding state:

1. one attributed label: Traced, Untraced, Disputed, or Beyond Sources;
2. exact Work identity and starting fingerprint from the typed directive;
3. heading when available, original line, and a short quotation from that revision;
4. observation or reconstruction;
5. why it matters;
6. evidential basis and access limit;
7. recommendation, with uncertainty.

The locator identifies an earlier version when the Work fingerprint has changed; never silently relocate the finding to newer wording. Keep observation, evaluation, and recommendation distinct. A proposed repair remains feedback until Write is separately authorized.

## Default output form

Use these sections in order unless the active researcher-owned Method explicitly replaces the form:

1. Overall Assessment
2. Strengths
3. Major Concerns
4. Source Support
5. Objections and Alternatives
6. Revision Priorities
7. Specific Findings
8. Materials Consulted and Limitations

An empty or inapplicable section may say briefly that no warranted content was established. Do not create praise, criticism, source confidence, or priorities merely to complete the form.
