# Critique method

## Establish the object

Bind the assessment to the supplied Work revision and whole-Work or passage scope. Reconstruct the relevant claim and its role before evaluating it.

## Independent lenses

Use only lenses that bear on the actual object:

- conceptual precision and stable definitions;
- argument validity, premise support, and inferential gaps;
- strongest objection, strongest available reply, residual pressure, and limits;
- source fidelity, source coverage, and missing evidence;
- alternative positions, cases, or framings;
- architecture, priority, and likely downstream consequence.

Do not force a criticism under every lens. Preserve genuine strengths and live alternatives.

## Finding form

For each material finding state:

1. one attributed label: Traced, Untraced, Disputed, or Beyond Sources;
2. exact Work identity and starting fingerprint from the typed directive;
3. heading when available, original line, and a short quotation from that revision;
4. observation or reconstruction;
5. why it matters;
6. evidential basis and access limit;
7. recommendation, with uncertainty.

The locator identifies an earlier version when the Work fingerprint has changed; never silently relocate the finding to newer wording. Keep observation, evaluation, and recommendation distinct. A proposed repair remains feedback until Write is separately authorized.

## Default output form

Use these sections in order unless the active researcher-owned Method explicitly replaces the form:

1. Overall Assessment
2. Strengths
3. Major Concerns
4. Source Support
5. Objections and Alternatives
6. Revision Priorities
7. Specific Findings
8. Materials Consulted and Limitations

An empty or inapplicable section may say briefly that no warranted content was established. Do not create praise, criticism, source confidence, or priorities merely to complete the form.
