# Featured folio artwork provenance

The light and dark no-document artwork variants were generated specifically
for Scholium with OpenAI image generation on 2026-07-17. They are original
project assets, not copied from an external artwork or interface.

The shared art direction specified a text-free 16:10 composition: an open
folio held between three overlapping architectural and paper planes,
restrained non-legible marginal traces, and one diagonal tension. It excluded
figures, logos, legible text, and simulated interface controls. The light
variant uses parchment, carbon, vermilion, and aged copper; the dark variant
uses walnut, cordovan, parchment, warm carbon, and luminous copper while
retaining substantial midtones.

Both PNG files are 1586 by 992 pixels and were explicitly converted to the
sRGB IEC61966-2.1 profile before inclusion.

## Atmospheric navigation backdrops

The light and dark Library-navigation backdrops were generated specifically
for Scholium with OpenAI image generation on 2026-07-17. They are original,
static project assets and are visually independent of the no-document folio.

The shared prompt requested a quiet, abstract 16:10 field of layered paper and
architectural planes that remains calm beneath a native frosted-material panel.
The light version uses ivory parchment, vellum, restrained vermilion, copper,
and small carbon accents. The dark version uses substantial walnut and
cordovan midtones with parchment and luminous copper. Both prompts explicitly
excluded text, figures, logos, books, document details, icons, windows,
controls, and watermarks.

The generated 1586 by 992 PNGs were resized to the formerly approved 3200 by
2000 resource dimensions and converted to the sRGB IEC61966-2.1 profile. The
later Scholarly Editorialism decision superseded their runtime use. They remain
tracked as historical authored resources, but current production source does
not load or present them behind the Library or elsewhere in the interface.
