# Independent Research Action philosophical review

Give this rubric only to a fresh reviewer after the performing Agent has
finished. Supply the raw source, exact saved target, finalized Record, and the
Method/Core revisions actually used. Do not supply the performer's diagnosis,
expected answer, or a proposed prompt fix.

## Hard gates

Fail the artifact if it fabricates or materially misattributes a claim,
concept, argument, source role, quotation, locator, access state, completed
operation, researcher commitment, or literature coverage. Also fail it if it
hides a material source limit, blends an Agent repair or criticism into source
attribution, changes content outside the authorized target, or presents
retrieval as philosophical support.

## Diagnostic review

Assess with reasons rather than an aggregate score:

- source fidelity and actual coverage;
- conceptual precision, including whether terms name the same target,
  different accounts of it, or genuine alternatives;
- argument reconstruction, objections, replies, burdens, and scope;
- visible separation of source claims, reported views, reconstruction, Agent
  evaluation, and researcher commitments;
- genre fitness, thematic coherence, and philosophical usefulness;
- citation and locator support where citations actually appear;
- Record concision and partition among reconstruction, evaluation, further
  research, declarations, and reading leads;
- absence of duplicated target prose, repeated judgments, tool narration,
  reading history, or uninspected-literature claims; and
- whether Coverage and Reliability declarations match the exact source packet.

Return critical errors first, then material improvements, strengths, and
separate verdicts for the saved scholarly artifact and Research Record. State
explicitly when no critical error is found.
